Customs Act 1967 Akta Kastam 1967

Laws of Malaysia · Act 235 Undang-Undang Malaysia · Akta 235

The principal customs statute — levy of customs duties, prohibition of imports and exports, offences, and enforcement powers. Undang-undang utama kastam — pengenaan duti kastam, larangan import dan eksport, kesalahan, dan kuasa penguatkuasaan.

Customs Regulations 2019 Peraturan-Peraturan Kastam 2019

P.U. (A) 397 · under the Customs Act 1967 P.U. (A) 397 · di bawah Akta Kastam 1967

Regulations made under section 142 of the Act — customs zones, warehousing, procedure, agents, and shipping agents. Peraturan yang dibuat di bawah seksyen 142 Akta — kawasan kastam, gudang, prosedur, ejen, dan ejen perkapalan.
